# Data card: `curated.jsonl`

## Summary

50 hand-written synthetic emergency-call transcripts, each labelled with
domain, severity, priority, cues, location status and victim count. This is
the platform's main development and held-out evaluation set. No real call
data is included or may be added (design v1.2, ADR-006).

## Why it exists

It measures the rules (B0), the models (B1, B1e) and their hybrids on
calls written outside the generator's templates. It targets the hard
cases: negation, past tense, hearsay, uncertainty, address corrections,
follow-up messages, Spanish, children and several victims.

## How it was made

- **`fx-*` (23):** ported from the earlier TypeScript fixtures and
  relabelled under the [labelling guide](../../../docs/evaluation/labelling-guide.md).
- **`cu-*` (27):** written as edge cases. The test-split `cu-*` cases were
  written after the rules parser and have not been used to change it.
- **Labels:** `label_source: "author"`. Every label was written by the
  project author, who also wrote the rules, so the labels are **not
  independent**. Independent annotation and adjudication follow the
  [annotation protocol](../../../docs/research/annotation-protocol.md).
- **Relabelled:** cases relabelled under guide v2.1 say so in their notes
  (`fx-014`, `fx-023`, `cu-116`, `cu-124`).

## Splits

Splits are assigned per family. `load_dataset` rejects a family that
appears in more than one split.
- `dev` may be inspected freely.
- `regression` gates CI.
- `test` is held out for pre-registered comparisons.

## Uses

- **Suitable:** development and regression checks of cue extraction and
  advisory priority, and held-out comparison of systems. Results come with
  counts and intervals.
- **Not suitable:** any claim of real-world dispatch accuracy, or training
  a model. The test split must never be used for tuning.

## Limitations

- 50 cases give wide intervals.
- The labels follow the project's research rubric, which no public-safety
  practitioner has reviewed.
- The author wrote both the rules and the labels.
- No label has evidence spans yet; span metrics need adjudicated labels.
- Spanish is the only language besides English.
